The perfect app to help you learn to sing

Our Artificial Intelligence algorithm removes the original vocals while keeping the melody of each song. That means you get access to a huge karaoke music library! As you sing, our algorithm analyzes your voice and pitch to accurately evaluate your performance and help you improve.

The largest karaoke music library!

Our AI removes the vocals from any song and gets it ready for you to sing, like magic!

A gamified experience

Your performance is scored using our ultra-precise algorithm. Challenge yourself, earn recognition, and celebrate your progress!

Record your performances

Whether you want to share your performances with friends or track your progress, our audio and video recording feature lets you make the most of the experience!

Share with the community

Share your recordings with the community, interact with others, and explore their vocal performances in your feed!

Real-time lyrics

Never lose the rhythm with synchronized lyrics displayed in real time. You can also turn on the original vocals for extra help.
